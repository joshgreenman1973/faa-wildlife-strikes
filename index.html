Federal Aviation Administration wildlife strike database Since 1990

Struck

In 2025 pilots and airports reported 24,459 wildlife strikes, about 67 a day.

The database holds 352,419 reports since 1990, from 2,788 airports, naming 975 species. Reporting is voluntary, so this counts what somebody wrote down, not everything that got hit. When only a smear is left, the Smithsonian's Feather Identification Lab names the species from the fragments, which is how a record can say Wilson's warbler at 32,000 feet. 12,494 of the strikes involved something other than a bird. 22,157 damaged the aircraft; those with a reported height are the dots below.
