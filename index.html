Federal Aviation Administration · National Wildlife Strike Database Since 1990

Struck

Every wildlife strike on an American aircraft that someone bothered to report since 1990. Reporting is voluntary, so this is not everything that has been hit — it is everything that got written down. When only a smear is left, the remains go to the Smithsonian's Feather Identification Lab, which names the species from fragments. That is why a record can say Wilson's warbler at 32,000 feet.
